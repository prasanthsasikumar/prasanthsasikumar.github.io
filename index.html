01 / Story

How I got here. Sense people well first, then build for them.

Haptics

2017 · HIT Lab NZ, Christchurch

My master's started with touch. I built a vibrotactile vest, a wind module and a rumbling floor, synced them to 360° film, and showed that feeling the scene significantly raises presence.

MSc thesis HIT Lab NZ 2018

Remote collaboration

2019 · Empathic Computing Lab, Auckland

Four depth cameras rebuild a worker's space live, so a remote expert can stand inside it and guide with their gaze and hands. This line of work became my PhD.

Wearable RemoteFusion ISMAR 2019 ↗Gaze + gesture sharing CHI 2020 ↗

Brain synchrony

2019 · Auckland

Two people drum together in VR while EEG measures how far their brains fall into step. An early look, with my lab, at inter-brain synchrony in shared virtual spaces.

HyperDrum SIGGRAPH Asia 2019 ↗NeuralDrum SIGGRAPH Asia 2020 ↗Sync + gaze CHI EA 2022 ↗

Volumetric capture

2021 · Auckland

Three calibrated Azure Kinects and a C++ stitching pipeline capture an expert in full 3D, then replay them in AR beside the learner's own workbench.

Volumetric playback Frontiers in VR 2021 ↗

Physiological sensing

2022 · PhD, Auckland

What if your partner could see your heart rate? I shared physiological cues between collaborators in VR assembly tasks and studied what it does to how pairs work together.

PSCVR ISMAR 2022 ↗Sharing physiological cues IEEE VR 2024 ↗

Motion sickness

2023 · NUS with Meta

With Meta Reality Labs we recorded people playing real commercial VR games to build VR.net, a large real-world dataset for predicting motion sickness before it hits.

VR.net IEEE TVCG 2024 Best paper ↗

AR training

2024 · NUS + Massey

HoloLens 2 training for fire evacuation and construction assembly, then a CHI study of the harder question: who should be in control, the AR system or the person?

AR fire safety Automation in Construction 2024 ↗User agency CHI 2025 ↗

Mixed-reality transitions

2025 · NUS

Zenflow slowly dissolves your real room into a night sky while guiding your breathing; a three-week field study showed better sleep quality and lower stress. The sky portal began as a hackathon trick at XR Hack Istanbul. CHI 2026 Honorable Mention.

Zenflow CHI 2026 Honorable mention ↗Co-designed stress MR ISMAR 2025 ↗

Body-aware MR

2026 · NUS with Asahi

EmoDrink reads physiological signals in mixed reality and suggests a drink for how you feel. A research collaboration with Asahi, shown at Augmented Humans 2026.

EmoDrink AHs 2026 ↗Code GitHub ↗

Fingertip haptics

2026 · NUS

PhantomFolds: two small actuators on a fingernail, driven in sequence, create a touch that seems to travel between them. Tactile feedback for mixed reality that leaves the fingertip free.

PhantomFolds IJHCI 2026 ↗

Attention-aware glasses

2026 · Smart glasses

Glasses that check whether your eyes or ears are free before they interrupt you, and route the message to whichever is free. Under 15 ms and under 1 MB, on device.

HeadRoom UIST 2026 ↗ChannelAwareGlasses GitHub ↗

Wearables that act

Now · FlowsXR, Singapore

Ten years of learning to sense people well, now turned into products for smart glasses that help people while they work.

02 / Building

Research, shipped. Products at FlowsXR, and tools I build in the open.

FlowsXR is a Singapore company building AI for smart glasses, which I founded and lead as CEO. The research above is why these products know what to sense.

flowsxr.com ↗

Sidekick

FlowsXR · on glasses

An AI assistant for field technicians: look at a machine and get step-by-step guidance. When the model is unsure, a human expert joins and sees what you see.

sidekick.flowsxr.com ↗

Hermes Glasses

Open source

A voice and vision assistant for Meta Ray-Ban Display and low-cost Realtek glasses: on-device transcription, the glasses camera, YOLO and OCR, and an agent bridge.

hermes-glasses GitHub ↗

TalkShot

FlowsXR · macOS

Push to talk, screenshot what you are looking at, say what is wrong, and hand it to your AI coding assistant. Designed glasses-first; live on macOS today.

talkshot.work ↗

PingIntro

FlowsXR · web

Digital business cards that scan paper ones in 100+ languages and remember who you met. Designed for glasses, live on phones today.

pingintro.com ↗

WASD

Open source

See how your interface reads through a Ray-Ban Display waveguide in daylight, composited over footage shot through real glasses.

WASD GitHub ↗

Anima Machines

Retired 2026

One photo becomes an expressive 3D character with voice, emotion and an LLM behind it, rendered in custom WebGL. Shown at Augmented Humans 2026, now retired with a gallery.

Gallery GitHub ↗
